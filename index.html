FAQ

Questions we hear most

Quick answers before you pick up the phone. Can't find yours? Just ask us.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes about 3–4 weeks. If you need it sooner, our fast-track option delivers in about 2 weeks for an extra 25%. The biggest factor is how quickly we receive your text, logo and photos, so having them ready saves days.

How much will my website cost?

Our Launch plan starts at ₹12,999 for up to 5 pages, and the Scale plan with an online store starts at ₹29,999. Custom web apps are quoted separately. Use the price calculator on the Pricing page for an instant range. After a short call we send a fixed quote, so there are no surprise costs.

What will hosting and a domain cost?

A domain name (like yourshop.in) and hosting are yearly costs paid to the provider, separate from the build price. As a rough guide, budget about ₹3,500–4,000 a year for a domain and hosting together, depending on your traffic. We recommend the right plan and set it up in your name, so you stay in control.

Will I own my website?

Yes. You get a clean handoff with full ownership of the code and content. There is no lock-in, and you are never forced to stay with us.

Can I update products and content myself?

Yes. Online stores come with easy product management, and we show you how to change text, prices and photos. If you would rather not touch anything, we can do the updates for you.

Will the website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site we build is responsive, which means it looks and works well on phones, tablets and computers. Most customers browse on their phone, so we design for mobile first.

Do you help after the website goes live?

Yes. Support is included for a period depending on your plan: 3 months with Scale and 12 months with Platform. Ongoing maintenance, such as updates, backups and monitoring, is available as a separate monthly plan.

How do payments work?

For larger projects, payment is split into milestones tied to design, build and launch. The exact schedule is written in your quote before we start, so you always know what is due and when.

How do I get started?

Send us a message on the Contact page, WhatsApp us, or book a free call. We reply within one business day, ask a few questions about your business, and send you a plan and a fixed price.

Still have a question? Contact us or message us on WhatsApp.
