FAQ

Pricing questions

Is this the final price?

These are starting points. After a short discovery call, we send a fixed quote based on your actual scope — no surprise costs later.

What's included in support?

Bug fixes, minor content updates, uptime monitoring and security patches for the period listed on your plan.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Most clients start with Launch or Scale and upgrade as their online store or product grows.

Do you offer payment plans?

Larger projects can be split into milestone-based payments tied to design, build and launch.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose Launch for a simple business site, Scale if you want to sell products or run a blog, and Platform for custom web apps and integrations. Not sure? Send us a message and we'll recommend one.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. They are yearly costs paid to the provider, roughly ₹3,500-4,000 a year together. We set them up in your name so you stay in control.
