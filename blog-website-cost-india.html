How much does a website cost in India?

A plain-language breakdown of what you pay for: the build, the domain, the hosting and the extras.

12 October 2026 · 6 min read · By Blyvax

"How much will a website cost?" is the first question most business owners ask, and the honest answer is: it depends on what you need. Here is a simple way to understand where the money goes, so you can compare quotes fairly.

1. The build (one-time)

This is the design and development work. The price depends mostly on how many pages you need and what the site must do. At Blyvax, our Launch plan starts at ₹12,999 for a site of up to 5 pages, and our Scale plan for a business with an online store starts at ₹29,999. Custom web apps, such as booking systems or dashboards, are quoted separately because every one is different.

2. Domain name (every year)

This is your web address, like yourshop.in. It is renewed every year, and the price depends on the extension you choose. It usually costs a small amount compared with the build.

3. Hosting (every year)

Hosting is the space where your website lives so people can open it. Prices vary with the provider and with how much traffic you expect. A small business site does not need an expensive plan. As a rough guide, budget about ₹3,500–4,000 a year for a domain and hosting together.

4. Extras that change the price

  • Online store and payments: product pages, a cart and a payment gateway
  • Blog or content management: so you can publish updates yourself
  • SEO setup: helping Google understand your pages
  • Logo and brand kit: if you do not have one yet
  • Extra languages or animations: nice to have, but not always necessary

5. Maintenance (optional)

Websites need updates, backups and security checks. You can handle this yourself or choose a monthly maintenance plan so someone keeps an eye on it.

How to compare quotes

  • Ask what is included: number of pages, revisions and support after launch
  • Ask who owns the code and the domain. It should be you
  • Ask for a fixed quote in writing, not a vague estimate
  • Be careful of prices that look too good to be true. Missing features usually cost extra later
The cheapest quote is not always the lowest cost. A slow or hard-to-update site can cost you customers every day.
