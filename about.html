Our story

Why we build the way we do

Blyvax was started by Sakshi Pardeshi, who kept seeing the same problem: businesses paying a lot for websites that were slow, hard to update, and never quite matched what they'd asked for.

So we built the studio around three habits — plain-language project plans, staging links you can check anytime during the build, and code we're not embarrassed to hand over when we're done. That's still how every project runs today.
