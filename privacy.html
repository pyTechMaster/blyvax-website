Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information, in plain language.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Blyvax ("we", "us") collects through blyvax.com, why we collect it and what choices you have. We are a web development studio based at Sai World Empire, Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

1. What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you are interested in, and the project details you write in the message.
  • Free website review: the website address you submit, your name, phone number, optional email, your goal and any notes.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: if you contact us this way, we receive the details you share (such as your number and message). WhatsApp is run by Meta and handles your data under its own policy.
  • Analytics (only if you accept): pages visited, buttons clicked, device and browser type, approximate location (city or country level), referring website and time on site. Microsoft Clarity may also record anonymised screen interactions such as scrolls and clicks.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme and cookie choice are stored in your browser so the site remembers them.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and discuss your project.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • To keep reasonable business records and to protect the site from spam and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visitors and see how the site is used. They set cookies or similar identifiers, and they load only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, they are not loaded. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the site, under their own privacy terms:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our email.
  • Google (Analytics, Maps and Fonts) and Microsoft (Clarity).
  • Our hosting provider, which serves the website.

We may also disclose information if required by law. Some of these providers process data on servers outside India.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to keep reasonable records of our communication with you, and delete them when they are no longer needed. Analytics data is kept according to the settings of the analytics tools.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your consent for analytics at any time. Write to us at sakshipardeshi705@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
