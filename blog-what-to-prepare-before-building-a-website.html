7 things to prepare before you hire a web developer

Get these ready first and your website will be built faster, cheaper and closer to what you imagined.

12 October 2026 · 5 min read · By Blyvax

The fastest way to a good website is to be ready before the work starts. Most delays happen because text, photos or decisions arrive late. Use this checklist and your project will move much more smoothly.

1. A clear goal

Write one sentence: "I want visitors to ___." Call you, order online, book an appointment or visit your shop. Everything on the site should support that one goal.

2. Your business details

Business name, address, phone number, WhatsApp number, email and opening hours. Check that they are correct and the same everywhere.

3. Your logo and brand colours

If you have a logo, keep the original file ready. If you do not have one, tell your developer early so it can be created as part of the project.

4. Photos and videos

Clear photos of your products, shop, team or past work make a big difference. Photos taken with a modern phone in good light are often enough. Avoid blurry images and pictures taken from other websites.

5. Your text

Write a short description of what you do, who it is for and why customers should pick you. It does not have to be perfect. A developer can help polish it, but it needs a starting point.

6. Websites you like (and do not like)

Share two or three sites you like and explain why. Examples help far more than long descriptions.

7. A budget and a timeline

Tell your developer your budget range and when you need the site. This helps them suggest the right plan instead of guessing. You can check a rough range with our price calculator.

Not everything has to be perfect before you start. A simple first version, followed by improvements, works better than waiting for everything to be ready.
