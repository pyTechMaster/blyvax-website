BRANDON: an online store for a local men's and kids' wear shop

An e-commerce website that lets a local clothing shop sell online, with a clean, fashion-first design that works on every phone.

E-commerceFashion
View live project

The challenge

A local shop relied on walk-in customers. It needed an online store that looks as good as the clothes, is easy to use on a phone, and does not need a technical team to run.

What we built

  • A bold, image-led home page with a brand banner and category shortcuts for casuals, formals and essentials
  • Product search, wishlist, cart and a customer account
  • A mobile-friendly layout, plus an install prompt for faster repeat visits
  • A free delivery offer on larger orders, shown at the top of every page
  • A WhatsApp chat button so shoppers can ask questions before they buy

The outcome

The shop now has its own online store that is open all day, alongside the physical shop. Customers can browse, save favourites and order without having to call.

  • A store that is open 24 hours, not just shop hours
  • Easy to browse and order on a phone
  • Questions answered quickly through WhatsApp chat
